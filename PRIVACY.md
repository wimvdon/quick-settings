# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

**This app collects nothing.**

- It does not collect or share any personal data.
- It has no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The
  "Download adb" button opens Google's download page in your browser; the app itself sends nothing.
- It reads and changes some of Android's own settings when you ask it to: brightness, screen
  timeout, always-on display, tap to wake, rotation, Night Light, extra dim, grayscale, color
  inversion, the ringer and volumes, touch vibration, pop-up notifications, USB and wireless
  debugging, animation speed, stay awake while charging, and Private DNS (mode and hostname).
- What it stores, only on your phone:
  - **Your defaults** (what is normal for each setting), whether each popup shows a
    notification, and the Private DNS hostnames you saved. Android's own backup may copy these
    to your Google account and to a new phone, like other app settings.
  - **Which of its tiles are in Quick Settings**, to show a check on the main screen. Not backed up.
  - Uninstalling the app, or Settings › Apps › Quick Settings › Storage › Clear storage, deletes both.
- Permissions, all used only for that:
  - **Modify system settings**: brightness, screen timeout, rotation and touch vibration.
  - **Write secure settings** (granted only with an adb command you run yourself): always-on
    display, tap to wake, the color settings, pop-up notifications, the developer settings and
    Private DNS.
  - **Notifications**: to show that a popup's settings differ from your defaults, with a button
    to put them back.

If this changes, this file will be updated **before** the change is released, describing what is
collected, why, where it is stored, and how to delete it.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
